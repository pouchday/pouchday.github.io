Shipping Restrictions

Because packages travel with real passengers on standard commercial flights, we must strictly adhere to TSA and international airline safety regulations.

Strict Zero-Tolerance Policy

Any package containing items from the lists below will be flagged, rejected, and will not be boarded onto the aircraft. Please double-check your items before packing.

Flammables & Aerosols

Pressurized cans or volatile liquids that could ignite under cargo hold pressure changes.

  • Aerosol sprays (deodorant, hairspray, body spray)
  • Nail polish and nail polish remover
  • Perfumes, colognes, and alcohol-based aftershaves
  • Lighters, matches, and lighter fluid

Lithium Batteries & Tech

Loose lithium batteries are classified as severe fire hazards in commercial air cargo holds.

  • Power banks and external portable chargers
  • Loose or spare rechargeable batteries (AA/AAA lithium)
  • E-cigarettes and vape pens
  • Smart luggage tracking tags with non-removable batteries

Perishables & Agriculture

Items subject to quick rot, spoiling, or strict international customs quarantine protocols.

  • Fresh fruits, vegetables, and meats
  • Homemade or unsealed snacks prone to bursting
  • Plants, seeds, and soil types
  • Liquids exceeding standard container structural thresholds

Weapons & Sharps

Any item that can be interpreted or classified as a security threat or hunting tool.

  • Pocket knives, utility blades, and scissors
  • Self-defense sprays (pepper spray, mace)
  • Tools exceeding 7 inches in overall length
  • Toy or replica firearms
